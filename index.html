How to claim

  1. Confirm your wallet holds a Binance Agent ID.
  2. Read the claim contract and verify the addresses above match Sourcify.
  3. Read currentFee() — it is 0 while the free quota remains, then 0.0002 BNB. Simulate the claim first (eth_call / preview). The simulation must show only the current fee leaving and 100,000 $GRIND arriving — no approvals, no other changes.
  4. Call claim() on the claim contract with value = exactly currentFee(). No token approval is needed.
  5. Verify on BscScan that 100,000 $GRIND arrived.

Questions

Is the claim free?

The first 500 claims are free — gas only. After the free quota is used up, each claim costs 0.0002 BNB plus gas. The live counter above shows how many free claims remain.

Can I claim twice?

No. One claim per wallet, enforced on-chain. A second call reverts.

What is $GRIND worth?

Nothing known. No pool, no price, no promise of a market. Claim only if you accept that.

Who runs this?

An anonymous builder. The contracts are public and verified; the terms above are the whole offer. Do your own research.
